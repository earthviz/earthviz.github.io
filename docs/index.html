How To Use Earthviz

Explore the planet in 3D, revisit Earth imagery from a chosen date, or look outward at the sky from any location.

Getting started

  1. Open the globe and drag anywhere on Earth to begin exploring.
  2. Search for a place or enter coordinates in the bar at the top.
  3. Choose Explore, Spacetime, or Stargaze to change what you are viewing.
  4. Open the panels on the left to adjust imagery, terrain, human elements, or sky conditions.
Earthviz streams imagery and terrain as you move. A newly visited area may take a moment to sharpen, especially at high detail.

Move around the globe

  • Pan: left-drag across the globe.
  • Orbit: right-drag around the selected surface point.
  • Free-look: middle-drag, or hold Shift while dragging.
  • Zoom: use the mouse wheel, trackpad, or the + and − buttons.
  • Reset: select the circular home control to return to the full globe.
  • Face north: select the compass control.

Select Focus in the lower-right controls to hide most of the interface. Select it again to restore the controls.

Find a place

Select the search bar and type a city, landmark, or location. Suggestions appear as you type; use ↑ and ↓ to move through them and Enter to choose one.

You can also enter decimal coordinates as latitude, longitude, for example 27.9881, 86.9250. Press Ctrl + K or ⌘ + K to focus search from anywhere.

Choose a viewing mode

Explore

Browse current satellite basemaps, terrain relief, roads, buildings, and live lighting.

Spacetime

Choose a date and inspect available NASA Earth-observation imagery for that day.

Stargaze

Look at the simulated sky from the selected location, date, and UTC time.

Explore

Choose yearly Sentinel-2 imagery, Esri World Imagery, NASA Blue/Black Marble, or the latest GOES/Himawari cloud views in Tiles. Terrain controls adjust surface detail, maximum zoom, relief, and mesh density. Roads and 3D buildings are optional and appear only when you are close enough to the ground.

Spacetime

Choose MODIS Terra, MODIS Aqua, VIIRS Suomi NPP, or VIIRS NOAA-20, then choose a date in the timeline and select View. Coverage depends on the satellite and imagery available for that day; black or empty areas can be genuine gaps. The lighting controls follow the selected date.

Stargaze

Drag to look around the sky and select a star to identify it. Set the UTC date and time in Sky conditions, or choose Today to return to the current time. The light-pollution control changes the faintest visible stars.

Map and sky controls

  • Tiles: select imagery and decide which visible tiles load first.
  • Terrain: balance visual detail against loading and rendering cost.
  • Human elements: show roads or 3D buildings near the surface.
  • Sky conditions: change observation time, animate day and night, inspect Sun and Moon events, and simulate light pollution.

Higher detail, finer terrain meshes, buildings, and roads can all increase loading time and graphics usage. If interaction becomes slow, reduce terrain detail or disable optional human elements.

Keyboard controls

  • W A S D move the camera.
  • Q and E move down and up.
  • Arrow keys change the viewing direction.
  • + and − zoom.
  • Hold Shift for faster keyboard movement.
  • Esc closes search suggestions and open panels.

Troubleshooting

The map is blurry or still loading

Pause movement briefly so high-priority tiles can finish. A slower connection, a high detail setting, or rapid travel between distant locations can increase loading time.

A layer is missing

Roads and buildings only appear near the ground and are hidden in Stargaze. Historical imagery can contain real coverage gaps. Try zooming out, changing date or imagery source, or returning to Explore.

The scene is dark

Lighting follows the active UTC time or selected historical date. Open Sky conditions, choose Today, or adjust the time slider.
