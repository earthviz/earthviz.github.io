About Earthviz ↗

Explore Earth in 3D for free

Earthviz is a free interactive 3D globe for exploring satellite imagery, detailed terrain, places, roads, and buildings around the world. There is no login, account, download, or installation required.

Browse historical NASA Earth observations in Spacetime mode, follow sunlight across the planet, or stargaze from any location and date. The project’s source code is available on GitHub.
